What this can and can't tell you

There is no true engagement rate.
Impressions are visible only to page admins, so rate here is engagement ÷ followers. Fine for comparing pages against each other and for tracking one page over time. Not comparable to the rate in your own page analytics.
Totals don't compare across handles; rates do.
Capture windows differ per handle, so the Benchmark sheet leads with posts/week, average engagement and the % commented / % reposted columns. Raw totals are in the Posts sheet for reference only.
Check the handles are the same kind of entity.
A global corporate page and a country subsidiary are not peers, however similar the brand. The workbook can't tell the difference — you have to.
Rows with zero engagement usually aren't dead posts.
They're posts whose counts hadn't rendered before the scroll passed them. Capture that handle again more slowly and drop both files in — posts dedupe by ID and the second pass fills the gaps.

Self-contained: open from disk or host it anywhere. The only external requests are the font stylesheet and the SheetJS library. Your capture files never leave the browser.
